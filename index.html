Three sisters, one farm, a district that needed both.

MDS Green Heaven was founded by Mpho, Dianah and Sharon Kutama to solve a problem they saw up close: Vhembe District's vegetables were either trucked in from Gauteng at a premium, or grown locally at the mercy of drought. Hydroponics offered a third way — production that doesn't depend on the rain, using a fraction of the water conventional farming needs.

We're registered, B-BBEE certified, and have secured a 20-hectare site in Makhado. What we're building now is the farm itself: greenhouse infrastructure, a nursery, a pack-house, and a team drawn from our own community.

Why hydroponics

The same harvest, on a fraction of the water.

Vhembe District's biggest constraint on agriculture isn't land or sunlight — it's water. Hydroponics lets us grow more, more reliably, with far less of the resource that's scarcest here.

Where we grow
